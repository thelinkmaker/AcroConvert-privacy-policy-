Privacy Policy for AcroConvert

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Privacy Summary

At AcroConvert, your privacy and the security of your documents are our top priorities. We are committed to protecting your personal data and only collect the minimum information necessary to provide our conversion services. When you use AcroConvert, we process your uploaded files strictly for the purpose of format conversion. We do not store, save, or retain any of your files on our servers. Your documents are processed and immediately discarded. We do not read, analyze, sell, or share the contents of your documents with any third parties. We do not use your files for any secondary purposes, including AI training or data mining. For any questions regarding your data or our privacy practices, please contact us at alhanhu@outlook.com.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize your privacy and collect only the minimal data required to operate the app. We do not collect the contents of the files you convert. We may collect basic, non-personal device information (such as device model and OS version) solely for the purpose of crash reporting and app performance optimization.

2. Zero File Retention Policy

AcroConvert operates on a strict zero-retention policy for user documents. Whether your files are processed locally on your device or temporarily processed in a cloud environment, no files are ever saved to our databases or servers. Once the conversion is complete and the file is delivered to you, the temporary data is immediately and permanently destroyed.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for essential app functionality, such as crash analytics (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) or payment processing (if applicable). These third-party providers have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. However, none of these services are given access to the contents of your converted documents.

4. Children's Privacy

AcroConvert does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
